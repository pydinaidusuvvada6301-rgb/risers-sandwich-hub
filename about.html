Who We Are

RISERS Sandwich Hub began with a single vision: bringing gourmet, artisan quality sandwiches to an accessible street-stall experience. We believe good food does not need pretentious packaging or exorbitant markups. It requires authentic ingredients, exact grill temperatures, house-crafted spice blends, and an uncompromising attitude toward freshness.

Whether it’s our succulent herb-roasted Chicken Sandwich (₹99) or our rich tandoori-spiced Paneer Sandwich (₹79), every single serving is built from scratch when you place your order.
